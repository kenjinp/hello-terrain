## Overview

A third-person character walking the surface of a ~5km radius cube-sphere
planet. Unlike the [flat character controller](/docs/examples/raycast-character-controller),
"down" is the radial direction toward the planet center, so gravity, ground
detection, and the follow camera all work in the sphere's local tangent frame.

### How it works

- **Local frame** — each frame the controller derives `up = normalize(position - center)`
  and a stable tangent basis (a "north" reference projected onto the tangent
  plane plus its perpendicular "east").
- **Movement** — WASD input is mapped into the tangent plane relative to the
  camera, and velocity is re-projected onto the tangent plane so the character
  slides along the sphere at a constant radius.
- **Gravity & jumping** — a scalar radial velocity is integrated along `up`,
  pulling the character toward the surface (and launching it on jump).
- **Ground detection** — a ray is cast radially inward with
  `terrainRuntime.raycast.pick(ray)`. On a cube-sphere surface this routes to
  the cube-sphere raycaster. `terrainRuntime.sphereQuery.getElevationByPosition(position)`
  is used as a fallback while the character is far above the surface.
- **Camera** — a follow camera orbits the character with `camera.up` aligned to
  the local surface normal; click the canvas to capture the mouse and look
  around.

The terrain LOD is centered on the character (via `getCameraOrigin`) so the
highest detail is always under foot, regardless of where the camera sits.
